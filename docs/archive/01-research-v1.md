# 01 — Research

> What we know about the problem, users, existing solutions, and the
> world outside the project. Findings belong here; assumptions and
> decisions should be clearly distinguished from them.

## 1. The Problem

### Working hypothesis

> People often know what they want to achieve, but turning that intention into concrete, timely action requires continuous planning, prioritization, and adjustment that they have to manage themselves.

This is a hypothesis, not a validated finding.

### Questions to investigate

- Who experiences this problem?
- When is it experienced?
- What do people do today instead?
- What solutions do they already use?
- Why are those solutions insufficient?
- Is this actually a meaningful problem?

## 2. Existing Solutions

The core idea is not new. Existing products cover areas such as goal planning, milestones, daily tasks, AI planning, replanning, coaching, and agentic planning.

The question is therefore not:

> "Has anyone thought of this?"

but:

> "How well is this problem already solved?"

### Known candidates

Goaltree, LifePilot, Mentra, Daysfold, Goaliath, GoalsFlow, Skedul.AI, Vakit, GoalOS.

### What to investigate

- Competitors
- Actual features, not marketing claims
- User experience
- Weaknesses and complaints
- Reviews
- User requests and feature requests
- What happens when plans fail
- How much work the user still has to do
