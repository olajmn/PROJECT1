This is a working application that explores how information can be organized in a persistent, visual structure and made available through a conversational interface.

The question behind it: can an explicit structure of information and its relationships make it easier for an AI to work with context over time, instead of having to reconstruct it from separate conversations?

Try this: open a project, click a part of the map, and ask the chat about it.

Your changes are deleted when you log out, after 30 minutes without activity, or after 8 hours at most.
